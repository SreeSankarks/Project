About Us

Who We Are

Yogakshema Sabha is a socio-cultural organisation of the Namboothiri community of Kerala. It was first founded in 1908 (as the Namboothiri Yogakshema Mahaasabha) with the aim of promoting education, social reform, and welfare within the community.

After a period of decline around the 1950s, the Sabha was reorganised and re-registered on 7 April 1976, with Thrissur as its focal centre, continuing its founding mission with renewed vigour.

Our mission is to support the social, financial, educational, and cultural development of our members — while preserving cultural values and encouraging community unity across generations.

Today, the Sabha operates through district-level committees (Jilla Samithis) across Kerala, each carrying forward local activities under the common banner of the Sabha.

Our Journey

History & Milestones

1908

A large gathering of Namboothiris met at Cherumukku Vaidikan's Illam on the banks of the Periyar river on Mahasivarathri day, giving birth to the Namboothiri Yogakshema Mahaasabha, focused on eradicating illiteracy and promoting modern education.

Early 1900s

The Sabha launched the monthly journal "Yogakshemam" and later "Mangalodayam Maasika", and established a library-cum-reading room at Thrissur that became a favourite place for researchers.

1942

The original Namboothiri Yogakshema Sabha, which had looked after community welfare in pre-independence days, was dissolved.

25 Dec 1975

A few Namboothiris met informally at the Namboothiri Vidyalayam, Thrissur, to discuss reviving the organisation for the community's general upliftment.

7 Apr 1976

Yogakshema Sabha was formally registered with Thrissur as its focal centre, open to all members of the Namboothiri community as defined under the Namboothiri Act of 1958.

28 Feb 1980

Nearly a thousand members marched from the University of Kerala to the Secretariat in Thiruvananthapuram, presenting a petition of 47 requisites to the government — a tradition of anniversary demonstrations that followed in later years.

1982

Permission was granted for a college at Sreekrishnapuram, a direct outcome of an earlier demonstrational procession organised by the Sabha.

Historical details are summarised from publicly available sources on the Sabha's history, including namboothiri.com. Exact details may vary slightly between regional chapters.
Our Purpose

Aims & Objectives

  • Promote the general social, financial, and cultural wellbeing of the community.
  • Assist members with the performance of traditional rituals ("Shodasakriya").
  • Encourage and support modern educational development among members.
  • Improve employment prospects and financial self-reliance for the community.
  • Preserve cultural values and spiritual traditions across generations.
  • Organise district-level conventions, family unions, and study classes.
  • Represent community interests before the government where needed.

About Stem Cell Donation

Stem cell (bone marrow) donation can be life-saving for patients with blood cancers and certain genetic disorders. Registering only requires a simple cheek swab — you're contacted only if you're found to be a match for a patient.

Eligibility: Generally age 18–50 and in good general health.
